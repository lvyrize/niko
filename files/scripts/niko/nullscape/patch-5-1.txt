Patch 5.1 "Delusional Descension" is a large update. It adds new tiles, a Garden biome, interior access to Highrise Tiles, and lobby modes Chaos+ and Celestial Practice; Fast Track skips early levels. Changed classes: Diver, Charger, Spirit, Grappler, Wanted. New Voidbound ICBM; Flesh no longer disables abilities. Many new curses, Greater Curses Ouroboros and Dimensional Beams, Overtuned Instruments returned. New Star Marts, Aggro Magnet, Jet Boosters, and Altars of Rerolls, Curses, Wishes, Duality. Scrapmaw restored to level 25.
