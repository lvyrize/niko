Defense and survival upgrades on the wiki include Helmet, Shield, Shadow Cloak, Subspacial Barrier, Final Stand, Health Insurance, Drowned Ægis, Panic Necklace, and Miniature Hourglass. Wanted cannot use Helmet, Shield, Shark Tail, or Miniature Hourglass. Shield and Subspacial Barrier gained visual indicators in Patch 5.1.
