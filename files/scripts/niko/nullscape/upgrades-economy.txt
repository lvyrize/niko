Economy and reward upgrades on the wiki include Paycheck, Business License, Medal, Tria Orbs, Star Marts (new in 5.1), Gift Magnet, Gift Idol, Altar Boons, More Altars, Magnified Levels, Birthday Pony, Blossom, Matrix Tetrahedron, and Pocket Bell.
