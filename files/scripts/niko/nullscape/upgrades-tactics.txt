radar, radar module, tactics upgrades, information upgrades
Information and tactics upgrades on the wiki include Radar, Radar Module: Enemies, Radar Module: Altars, Radar Module: Instruments, Radar Module: Tripmines, Radar Module: Players, Soul Tracers, Defuse Kit, Orb, and Adrenaline.
