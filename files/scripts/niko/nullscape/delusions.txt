delusion, delusions, altar of duality
Delusions are run modifiers that mix upgrades and curses, with both positive and negative effects. They appear in intermissions if the Altar of Duality was triggered in the previous level. A run can have at most 3 active Delusions. The Altar of Duality can remove them if the Altar of Wishes' effect is active, and the next intermission then lets the player remove one chosen Delusion. Base Delusions: Assurance, Corrosion, Desire, Greed, Hope, Perseverance. Unlockable by achievements: Arrogance, Burnout, Conceit, Dependence, Gambling, Hoarding, Ignorance, Overconfidence, Vandalism.
