Three difficulties set enemy behavior, event timing, and Tripcoin rewards. Casual: 2 starting Upgrades, curses every 3 levels, no Tripmines, 0.8x Tripcoins. Standard (unlocked at level 15 on Casual+): enemies on level 1 and even levels, Tripmines from level 5. Extreme (also unlocked at level 15): enemies every level, Tripmines from level 3, 1.5x Tripcoins, always-active Tripnuke. Hard was renamed Extreme in Patch 5. Solo on Extreme uses Standard-style enemy selection. Chaos and Party are not covered on the wiki.
